## The poem

> For years I kept my hands in my pockets,
> called it patience. Called it pride.
> One night I knocked — just once —
> and the door swung open on oiled hinges.
> It had never been locked.
> All that time, the yes had been standing
> on the other side, waiting to be asked.

The turn is the whole poem: years of waiting on a permission that was never
withheld. The door was never locked; only the asking was missing.
